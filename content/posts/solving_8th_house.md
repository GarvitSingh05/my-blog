## 1. Introduction
#### 1.1 Moon's Debilitation in 8th House Correlated with Mother
Moon, the karaka of mother gets debilitated in 8th house, so 8th house has negative karma associated with mother, there will always be some kind of issues of abandonment from the mother. When 8th house becomes prominent in the chart, it gives that.

---

When a person has powerful planets in the 8th house of their chart, or 8th lord gets powerful, I call it the 'Karna' placement. If we look at the life of Karna from the Mahabharat, it completely resonates with 8th house themes. Karna is an 8th house character depicted perfectly in the Mahabharat. Themes surrounding his entire life give us deep insights into how the 8th house works.

## 2. Karna's Backstory
#### 2.1 Early Life
If you read the Mahabharat, you will come to know that very early in his life, he was abandoned by his own mother Kunti, who feared that she will not be accepted in traditional society if she happens to have a child before marriage. Karna was the son of Lord Surya, and he had been gifted a special protective armor and a pair of earrings by his father. He was raised by a stepmother and a stepfather who belonged to the caste of charioteers.

---

#### 2.2 Karma with Mother
This shows the 8th house has karma of not receiving the love of your mother, feeling abandoned by her and being at a distance from her, be it physically or emotionally. Kunti chose to prioritize herself over Karna. Instead of owning up to her mistake, she avoid taking any accountability, which led to her abandoning Karna into Maa Ganga.

---

#### 2.3 Eldest Son Karma
Being born as the eldest son in his family, he could have easily become the undisputed king of Hastinapur, but he was abandoned, his rights taken away from him and he grew up to be the son of a charioteer, and he had to go through a lot of humiliation because of it.

This is what the misfortune theme of 8th house is. Even after deserving the position of the king, he still had to settle with less. Had to live in compromised conditions. He had capability, but he was always challenged and opposed by people due to his caste of a charioteer. He was insulted and humiliated because of his caste.

The natural 8th house of kaalpurush has Scorpio sign, which has Jyestha nakshatra, which shows karma related to being the eldest son of the family who is subjected to a lot of extreme situations and has to compromise for the betterment of his family.

---

#### 2.4 Knowledge Should Be Accessible To All, Regardless of Caste
His idea was that knowledge should not be limited to a certain caste. Knowledge should be accessible to everyone, regardless of their caste. If one is willing to learn, caste should not become a barrier in getting knowledge. He was the son of a charioteer but wanted to become a warrior, a kshatriya, and due to this, he was constantly ridiculed by everyone, despite his extraordinary capabilities in warfare.

---

#### 2.5 Going Against the Outdated Norms of Society
This is the theme of 8th house where people hate you because you are wanting to make a great change by going against the current set norms of society which are outdated and do not serve any good purpose.

---

#### 2.6 Supporting The Unrighteous
Karna had good supportive friends, but even they sometimes had misunderstandings with him, there was a tussle about loyalty. People misunderstand and underestimate you in 8th house. You should not trust your friends too much when you have a powerful 8th house.

Karna, no matter how many good karmas he would have done, he was still standing for the wrong side and he never spoke against whatever Duryodhan did.  He was bounded by the promises he made to his friend Duryodhan due to which he never opposed him, and that is why his prarabdha was such that he was killed in the Mahabharat.

When you have prominent 8th house, standing by the side of anything unrighteous and not speaking up against it will add up to your bad karmas. What is more important to you: loyalty to someone or following dharma, you decide.

---

#### 2.7 Giving Nature - Letting Go of Things
Karna was known as 'daanveer' and this quality was used against him by gods like Indra to take away his armor and earrings. People take undue advantage of you because of your giving nature when you have lord lagna connected to 8th house. This person's giving nature becomes a curse for him. People will take advantage of this quality of giving in him.

---

#### 2.8 Respect, Recognition & Fame After Death
Karna from the time he was born, lived a life of suffering, all throughout his life, he wasn't given the due credit, it is only after his death that he was given proper respect. When you have 8th house powerful, people will respect your contributions to society only after you have died. Till the time you are alive they will be ignorant about you, they will not give you your due credit. This also makes you leave a strong legacy for sure, but all the fame and recognition from society comes only after you die. That is the powerful 8th house, the Karna placement.

## 3. 8th House Themes - Derived By Studying Karna's Life
Karna's character was a depiction of all the 8th house themes. He wanted to make a change, which is why he was hated by everyone. This is how the 8th house is. The entire life will be a suffering, but in the end there is victory for sure. You will be abandoned, humiliated, you will not be given due credit for your contributions, people will take advantage of you due to your giving nature, you loyalty will be questioned by the very people whom you loved, all this is very painful, but in the end, just like Karna was granted eternal glory by all the gods, this person will be remembered for generations to come, he will be remembered for making a great change.

---

#### 3.1 Parivartan
8th house is the house of a person who wants to bring 'Parivartan'. People translate 'Parivartan' as 'transformation' or 'change'. But 'Parivartan' actually means 'reforming'. 8th house is the house of reform, not transformation. All the people who have reformed the society for the better in some way or the other will have a powerful 8th lord.

People who rely on google translate struggle to understand the real meaning of the word 'Parivartan'. Translation will tell you that Parivartan means transformation. This is what most people believe nowadays that 8th house means transformation. But the word 'Parivartan' here has been used with a connotation that 8th house means the reforms that one does for the betterment of society.

8th house is the house of reform, not transformation. If you have a powerful 8th house or 8th lord, then you are the chosen one by god to make a great change for the betterment of humankind. If you do not have a powerful 8th house, then you haven't really seen what struggle and pain feels like.

---

#### 3.2 Legendary Status
People who are ultra-successful in their lives, one thing is common about them, and that is a powerful 8th house. Certain examples include:

1. Albert Einstein had exalted mars in 8th house with rahu.
2. Elon Musk has 8th lord mars exalted in 5th house with rahu.
3. MK Gandhi had 8th lord Venus in Mooltrikona sign in 1st house.
4. Robert J Oppenheimer had Sun Mars Mercury in Aries in 8th house, which means exalted Sun and mooltrikona sign Mars in 8th house.
5. Warren Buffett had exalted Jupiter in 8th house and see what he achieved in terms of finances.
6. Rakesh Jhunjhunwala had Mars in Aries in 8th house.
7. Osho Rajneesh had his 8th lord Jupiter exalted in 3rd house.
8. IPS Manoj Sharma, on whom the movie '12th Fail' was made, he has Mars in Aries in 8th house.

These are just some of the people. All the revolutionary and legendary people ever born, they will have a powerful 8th house, a powerful 8th lord, or any powerful planet connected to their 8th house or 8th lord. Their life will be full of suffering, since 8th house is getting stronger, but in the end, the reforms they do for the world, they are remembered by generations to come. See your own chart, if you do not have a powerful 8th house and 8th house lord, then no matter how many good ideas you have, no matter how much you work, you cannot bring parivartan, you cannot reform society in the way a person with a powerful 8th house can do.

---

#### 3.3 Fame By 8th & 10th House - The Difference
You need a powerful 8th house if you want to be remembered like a legend for generations to come. 10th house fame and power is such that it works only till the time you have your title, and the day you loose that title, all your power is now over. 10th house means the power that keeps transferring from one king to another. You serve as the king, complete your term and then you step down. When you step down, someone else will be given the crown that you had, the title which was yours now belongs to someone else, all the glory you felt after having that title, now it is all gone. This is what the 10th house type of fame and power is. 

8th house gives you that kind of recognition which stays even after you have died. People remember you for the things you have done even after your death, that is given by the 8th house. 8th house is the type of wealth you accumulate that stays with you forever. In 10th house, the power and glory was temporary, but whatever you gain in the 8th house, it stays with you forever, and people will remember you for that even after you gone. A powerful 8th house gives a strong legacy. This person will have a great impact on people. This is the Parivartan aspect of 8th house.

---

#### 3.4 Suffering
Let me tell you, it might seem like people with powerful 8th house are so lucky, but you cannot even imagine the pain and suffering that they will have to go through in their life. 8th house has very strong negative karmas where a person feels neglected by their mother, feels outcasted from society, is hated by people around him, is misunderstood. Due to all this, 8th house also gives you conflict, which is both internal and external. Internal conflict feels like there are a billion thoughts going on in your head at the same time and you feel like my head is going to explode. Internal conflict is faced by everyone at some point of their lives, but it is the strong 8th house person who will be able to make sense of it and produce something good out of it. Rest of the people will suffer and nothing good comes out of it.

Sufferings are there in everyone's lives. A person with strong 8th house will be blessed after going through the sufferings, but a person with weak 8th house will keep on suffering and feel helpless against fate or destiny.

---

#### 3.5 Problems From Females
One more thing about 8th house, it has karma, not just with your mother, but in general with females. These people will have problems from them. Males will have problems with females and females will have problems with other females. You need to watch out. Also, nowadays this can also include even a male who is very feminine in nature, you need to be careful from such people.

It doesn't mean every female you meet in your life will try to create problems for you, but there will be certain females that you encounter who will have enmity towards you for no reason when you have a powerful 8th house.

---

#### 3.6 Mental Health Problems
All the suffering in life which a powerful 8th house gives can make this person vindictive, revengeful, sadistic, melancholic and ruthless in nature. This needs to be checked. Either it will give this vindictive nature or it can also make the person loose all hope and make them suicidal. Mental health struggles a lot when you have a powerful 8th house.  

---

#### 3.7 Suppression
8th house has the themes of suppression. The reason why I say this is that Karna, all throughout his life, he was eager to display his talents, to prove to everyone that he is the greatest warrior, but there were limiting factors all around him, he went all throughout Mahabharat, he had all the abilities but never got to use them. Sometimes he was stopped by certain promises, sometimes dharma, sometimes by his elders. He had all the talent and capability and he was trying his entire life to showcase it, but never got a proper chance.

His mother Kunti had taken a promise from him, that he will go only after Arjun and not the other pandavas. Whenever he used to fight them, at some point, he had to back off because if he showed his true powers, the pandavas like Bheema etc. will die, and when he used to back off, he was insulted by them as they called him a suta-putra and a coward.

8th house has a very suppressive quality to it. The person has to live in a very compromised situation, there will be limiting factors around him that will sabotage his progress. This is what 8th house does.

It has a very suppressive quality, where a person with powerful 8th house has a lot of stored potential energy in him, which will remain dormant in early part of life, but one day it will explode and then you will see the steep rise to success and power.

Powerful 8th house person is like Vamana, people will underestimate him, but one day he will rise above all challenges and rise very high. People will be shocked to see what you are capable of.

We get diamonds from 8th house which are buried deep within earth. Diamonds are created under tremendous pressure. So will this person be. He will be suppressed like anything, he will feel the pressure but that pressure will only turn him into a diamond. Remember, all these good results will happen only when 8th house is powerful, otherwise not.  

---

#### 3.8 Unsupported & Sabotaged By Father
8th house is 12th from the 9th house of father. 8th house being prominent does mean that the person is not supported by his father, or fatherly figures. Sometimes, they even try to sabotage this person's progress in life, they will try to stop him from progressing.

I have already written about karma related to mother where these people are abandoned by their mother, be it literally physical, or even on a mental and emotional level. It can be scenarios where either mother dies early, or lives separate, or mother doesn't love you, she loves and favours your siblings more than you. 8th house has a strong karma of not receiving that level of motherly love which any normal child is supposed to get.

---

##### 3.9 "The Entire World Is Against Me"
8th house, will at one point make you feel like the entire world is going against me. Mother doesn't love, father doesn't support, children oppose you, spouse doesn't understand you, it can be an extremely difficult place to be. Karna's step father tried to stop him from becoming Angaraj, and then Bhisma told him to stay out of the Kurukshetra war for I think 9 days. His real father Lord Surya never told him the reality about his parents and that he had been living with his step parents. He kept Karna in the dark for so long, and Karna felt betrayed and unsupported by his own father. This is the aspect of your elders, your father figures trying to sabotage you from doing what you always felt like you were born to do. Problems with both mother and father are quite prominent when you have a powerful 8th house.  

---

#### 3.10 Opposition From Children
8th house being prominent also means children themselves can run into problems, they can oppose you at some point. It will be devastating for sure. At least with children you expect they will listen to you, but there can be circumstances where your children might rebel and go against you. Need to check the exact condition in the chart before saying anything with surety. Karna in the story, his son attacked him because he was supporting his friend Sambh.

The children of a person with powerful 8th house are highly likely to go on a wrong path, and when this person tries to stop them, he will be opposed by those children. The karmas with children here are not as bad as they were with the parents. The mutual love between you and your children will still be fine, it's just that they will try to do their own thing, and you might not like it.

---

#### 3.11 Mrityunjaya Vardaan
Life is going to be full of bitter experiences when you have a powerful 8th house. Karna was an 8th house character, and he was given the vardaan of Mrityunjaya, means the one who defies death. That is why I say that 8th house is capable of making you so successful that people remember you even after you are dead for generations to come. 10th house fame dies as soon as you loose your position, but the 8th house has been given the Mrityunjaya vardaan.

Karna struggled all his life to earn this. It is because of his efforts that 8th house was given this quality of being Mrityunjaya. 8th house makes your name immortal. It is because of Karna that 8th house has been granted this power where people will remember you name for generations, they will remember what you contributed to society for centuries. This is what the Mrityunjaya vardaan does. And this vardaan was earned by Karna.

It is because of his struggle that people nowadays who have a powerful 8th house experience this kind of success, name and fame. He fought for parivartan his whole life and earned this Mrityunjaya vardaan for everyone having a powerful 8th house. If you have a powerful 8th house, then be grateful to him, he gave his entire life, and his life was full of misery, but he gave you some extraordinary capabilities in 8th house.  

---

#### 3.12 Supported By Friends, But Don't Put Too Much Trust
With powerful 8th house, you will have good support of friends, but don't trust them too much as they will be the ones contributing to your downfall. Their actions can bring you misery. Best not to have too many friends. A small circle of good friends should be the way you approach friendships in your life.

---

#### 3.13 Humiliation
Karna was humiliated in the Swayam Var by Draupadi. People frequently used to humiliate him due to his caste.

8th house signifies humiliation. Why does humiliation even occur? Humiliation is felt when you feel insecure and you try to prove your point or prove your worth to someone.

When you have a powerful 8th house, don't try to prove anything to anyone about anything. Don't be insecure. Be self confident and self assured. If you believe you are the best at a certain thing, then accept it confidently and don't try to prove to everyone that you are the best. It just reveals that you are insecure from inside when you try hard to prove anything to anyone.

If you are the best at something, and if you have a powerful 8th house, people will naturally come to know about your strengths and they will like you, but if you try to go against the natural flow and forcefully you go to everyone and tell them about what all you can do.

All this basically means that you are doubtful of your abilities, you are insecure from inside and you are trying to seek validation from outside. You are basically trying super hard to just get people to say that yes, you are actually good.

8th house prominent people, many a times in life, they will feel ignored and neglected, and this makes them do all of this. Let people discover your natural talents and powers, don't go up to each one of them in this hope that they will recognize your capabilities. It will just invite more and more insults, disrespect and humiliation.

These people are so deprived of the basic level of appreciation that they try to go out of the way to get it themselves. It will not work. You have to let things unfold in the manner destiny has meant it to be.

The reason why I say all of this, all throughout Mahabharat, Karna was insulted by being called a sutaputra, a lower caste. His abilities were questioned. Now tell me, if Karna was very self assured, that I know I am the best archer in the universe and I don't need to prove it to anyone, If I believe it, no one else needs to.

But he was not like this, internally he used to feel insecure when he used to hear from everyone that Arjun is the best. Internally, he felt inferior and insecure. That is why he sought out to challenge him in battles, he was trying to prove to himself and to the entire world that he was the best.

But really, if you are actually the best, you need not try to prove it to anyone. Now, these are very harsh lessons. I am not trying to say Karna was wrong. All such behaviour often arises out of the deep internal conflict that a person has to go through with a powerful 8th house. I have never seen someone as strong will powered as him. Of course he was the son of Lord Surya, he had to be strong will powered.

The lesson is do not try to prove anything out of fear, insecurity or inferiority complex. Don't try to pretend to be anything, be your natural self.

A lot of people in modern world today find inspiration with his character and his life.

When you have a powerful 8th house, more than refraining from doing bad karma yourself, you have to be careful, whenever you see something wrong happening around, you should stand against it. Don't be that person who sees something wrong and takes no action, thinking that it doesn't have anything to do with me.

## 4. Other Themes Of 8th House
